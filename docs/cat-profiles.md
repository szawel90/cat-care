# Cat profiles and observation portraits

A signed-in owner can create a cat with only a name and optionally upload a photo. The account page lists cat cards; the centered header picker always offers another cat. Creating a profile opens that profile without starting an interview. The compact profile combines identity, behavioral descriptions and six area cards. Life events and change history are separate collapsed sections. Health is disabled until its dedicated module is available. Home, routine and preferences use structured choices with optional notes, preserving earlier free-text values until explicitly changed. Household assignment is edited from My home.

Cats may share a household or belong to separate households. Explicitly choosing a housemate reuses household facts and physical environment details. Individual behavior, access limitations, health and preferences remain specific to the cat. Declared animal counts include animals without profiles: registration alone must not count the same animal twice. Archiving a profile preserves its household membership; a household move is a separate action.

## Observation rules

The `cat-portrait-scenarios-2` rules produce nine coordinates from at most 18 base questions, or 16 without an observable other-cat relationship, and at most two clarification slots per observation. The rules and short questionnaire are an authored research draft, not a validated psychometric or diagnostic instrument. No language model calculates scores or fills missing answers. Previous results remain stored under their original rule version; a new version requires a new observation cycle.

Most questions describe a naturally occurring scene and ask for the observed response; Q04 retains frequency choices. Authored ordinal mappings use 0–4 for the relevant behavior, without interpreting them as severity or population percentiles. Leaving and hiding both indicate withdrawal; tense immobility remains contextual evidence. Answer later combines uncertainty and lack of opportunity into a pending observation. Context-dependent behavior and route-inapplicable questions remain distinct and never become zero. A pair differing by at most one point is averaged; larger differences remain a range without a fabricated midpoint. Missing paired indicators remain explicitly preliminary. Household associations can prevent a context-independent position; spatial preferences require real alternatives and reported relaxed rest. Relationship coordinates concern the same identified other cat, not an aggregate household personality.

The interface shows several behavioral names and a short deterministic description, without numeric scales or percentages. Internally, all nine coordinates, ranges, missing positions and context remain available. Named bands use points up to 1, at least 3, and the intermediate region; ranges, context and observed spatial use have separate descriptions. These are application thresholds, not thresholds established by the books. The stored `cat-description-1` semantic keys identify the bilingual phrase library and supporting questions. Combined descriptions preserve simultaneous closeness and caution, or affiliation and distancing. Missing indicators cannot support a sentence claiming both paired behaviors. A changed context is flagged without recalculating historical results or descriptions. Future phrase-library changes require a new description version.

Single-answer buttons save and advance on activation; focus alone does not submit. Multi-selection requires confirmation. The next question receives focus and scroll position. The bottom row separates Return to profile from Answer later. Continuation visits only applicable missing or deferred answers, and visits a deferred answer at most once per continuation. Newly invalidated dependencies can be asked again. Answering within the original period creates a new revision. Later observations start a new period and do not copy behavioral answers. Only confirmed household context may prefill Q02. Editing prerequisite answers invalidates dependent current responses while preserving earlier revisions. Previously used clarification slots remain spent after a correction.

## Versioned domain data

`Cat` and `Household` hold identity, current pointers and lifecycle metadata. `CatVersion`, `HouseholdVersion` and `PortraitRevision` preserve collected payloads. Changes append records inside transactions and update only previous-version flags and timestamps. Compare-and-swap version checks reject lost updates with HTTP 409. Observation dates are separate from recording dates; results retain the rule version and household snapshot used to produce them.

Cat archival is reversible. The existing permanent account-deletion process cascades through all owned history and photos. Account export includes all domain versions. Every endpoint authenticates the owner, verifies ownership and uses `no-store`; writes require the trusted origin. Photos are size-limited, decoded and re-encoded to strip attached metadata, and served through authenticated routes.

The visible interview history groups revisions by assessment ID, including answers recorded on different days. Each cycle shows its start, last update and latest status. The underlying revisions remain available for historical results and export. The same append-version pattern applies to future important domain records. Operational account credentials and sessions continue to follow the existing authentication lifecycle.

## Verification

The test suite includes historical offline-reference fixtures, scenario mappings, bilingual catalog parity, missing and contradictory evidence, conditional continuation, cycle grouping, structured choices, household reuse, ownership boundaries, concurrent updates, photo validation, immutable history, archive/restore and account-deletion cascades. Browser tests exercise creation, automatic saving, failed-save recovery, question focus, nonadjacent deferred responses, household changes and historical views. Storybook covers picker, compact profile and portrait states with keyboard and accessibility checks. These checks do not validate behavioral accuracy or establish full accessibility conformance.
